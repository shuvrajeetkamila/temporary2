4. TRUE 1:1 SCALE, CRS, AND FLOATING ORIGIN
-------------------------------------------

Use true 1:1 local metre scale.

- One projected metre equals one game metre.
- Human/vehicle/building/road/runway/causeway/port dimensions remain physically meaningful.
- Do not compress islands together.
- Do not stretch runways or causeways.

Master coordinates:

- Store global positions in double precision.
- Default to WGS84 / UTM zone 39N (EPSG:32639), which covers Bahrain, after verifying source metadata and checkpoints.
- If an authoritative Bahrain source uses another national grid/CRS, perform a documented tested conversion into one runtime master CRS.
- Verify axis order, units, datum, false easting/northing, and transform.
- Latitude/longitude remain metadata/conversion inputs, never physics units.

Runtime coordinates:

- GLES/Bullet use local float coordinates near active focus.
- Implement floating origin because the archipelago/water/airspace/causeway routes span large distances.
- Every entity keeps authoritative global double transform plus local float transform.
- Origin shift updates terrain, water phase anchor, collision, vehicles, human, camera, particles, decals, audio, AI, traffic, buses, boats, wildlife, constraints, and streamed props coherently.
- Chunk requests use immutable global keys so stale pre-shift local data cannot apply.

Origin tests:

- Human walking.
- Expressway-speed car.
- Boat crossing island groups.
- Helicopter to Hawar/remote areas.
- Aeroplane country circuit.
- Active suspension/gear/rotors/propeller/constraints.
- AI routes and bus progress.
- Save/load at country extremes.
- No visible teleport beyond documented tolerance.
- No velocity/force/phase discontinuity.

Chunk lifecycle:

- UNLOADED.
- REQUESTED.
- QUEUED_IO.
- DECODING_CPU.
- READY_CPU.
- QUEUED_GPU.
- ACTIVE_HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW.
- EVICT_PENDING.
- EVICTED.
- FAILED_OPTIONAL/FAILED_REQUIRED.

Selection considers:

- Global focus/camera.
- Altitude.
- Speed/heading.
- Vehicle mode.
- Stopping/turn radius.
- Boat water route/shore obstacles.
- Flight approach/departure.
- Quality/thermal profile.

Rules:

- Coarse all-islands terrain/coast/city massing available globally under strict budget.
- Preload faster/farther for aircraft and boats than walking.
- Collision loads before decorative geometry.
- Bounded worker threads and cancellation.
- GPU uploads on GL thread with per-frame time/byte budget.
- Shared asset reference counts.
- Hysteresis prevents thrash.
- Occupied-vehicle collision cannot evict.
- Analytic sea global; detailed shore/port/island chunks streamed.
- Required terrain/coast/collision failure shows safe error; no invisible hole.

      fixedDt = 1.0 / 60.0

      interpolationAlpha = accumulator / fixedDt

BAHRAIN-SPECIFIC ANNEX A — REGIONAL PRODUCTION BIBLE

This annex is mandatory. It prevents one procedural city/desert grammar from being copied across the archipelago. Every region has a data-driven identity profile. Profile values select materials, vegetation, facade families, prop sets, traffic density, ambient audio, night lighting, terrain microdetail, coastline treatment, and LOD priorities. They never override authoritative geometry.

A1. MANAMA HISTORIC/COMMERCIAL CORE
-----------------------------------

Geographic character:
- Dense low/mid-rise fabric mixed with modern commercial towers.
- Flat coastal/reclaimed terrain; no hills invented around the old core.
- Tight streets, service lanes, major arterials, waterfront/reclaimed edges according to epoch.
- Continuous relationship to Manama Souq, Bab Al Bahrain, government/commercial districts, and modern skyline.

Visual language:
- Warm pale plaster/stone/concrete in older fabric.
- Shaded arcades, awnings, recessed shopfronts, roof parapets, external AC/HVAC clutter, water tanks only where source/archetype supports them.
- Modern glass/steel towers use individually authored silhouettes and low-cost distant emissive-window masks.
- Ground-level dust, salt/humidity staining, traffic wear, and strong shade contrast.
- Do not make every older wall pristine beige or every tower blue mirrored glass.

Street life:
- Higher pedestrian/commercial density at suitable evening periods and shaded lanes.
- Generic original shop signs in valid Arabic/English; factual place names only.
- Delivery vans, taxis, buses, private cars, motorcycles, pedestrians, parked vehicles, street furniture, waste bins, bollards, shade, and loading activity under agent/prop budgets.
- No copyrighted brands, recorded market music, or fake Arabic.

Audio:
- Bounded traffic bed, footsteps, doors, ventilation, distant harbor/city hum, birds, and original market ambience.
- No perpetual horn wall and no country-stereotype sound loop.

QA shot/route:
- Approach from a modern arterial into older streets, walk through an exterior souq route, emerge toward the waterfront/modern skyline.

Tier 4 — deterministic inference:
- Used only where legal source lacks noncritical attributes.
- Every inferred field carries rule/version/confidence.
- Never infer political boundaries, heritage-site extents, restricted interiors, or operational details.

Rejected sources:
- Google/Apple/Bing proprietary map or imagery captures.
- Street View screenshots.
- Ripped games/models.
- Unlicensed social-media/photos/scans.
- GADM or other data when its license conflicts with APK redistribution/commercial status.
- A tourism/master-plan rendering treated as current built geometry.

B2. FEATURE FALLBACK MATRIX
---------------------------

National boundary missing/conflicting:
- STOP. Do not invent. Resolve with consistent licensed authority and document decision.

Island name missing:
- Keep stable generated source ID; do not invent public-facing name. Display “Unnamed island” only in developer diagnostics unless reviewed.

Building height missing:
- Infer from mapped levels; otherwise footprint/use/zone deterministic distribution with conservative caps and confidence flag.

Building style missing:
- Zone archetype selected from land use/settlement/epoch; generic local-compatible style, never landmark clone.

Road lanes/width missing:
- Infer from highway class/one-way/context using a versioned Bahrain road-default table; preserve uncertainty.

Arabic name missing:
- Use reviewed official/open transliteration source when available; otherwise English only in shipped UI rather than machine-generated nonsense.

English name missing:
- Reviewed transliteration from Arabic; source/rule recorded.

Terrain elevation void:
- Interpolate only small voids; larger/critical Jabal/runway/coast voids fail validation or require another legal source.

Bathymetry missing:
- Conservative generated depth from coast distance/open contours/channels; label game-generated and never real navigation data.

Vegetation missing:
- Land-use/biome procedural sparse placement with regional caps; no dense palms outside supported parcels.

Heritage geometry missing:
- Preserve mapped footprint/site boundary with generic respectful massing or marker. Never fabricate detailed reconstruction.

Restricted facility detail missing:
- Good; use generic exterior/no-entry. Never infer.

Bus schedule missing:
- Generic ambient buses/known mapped stops only. Do not invent an official timetable.
