3. SCALE AND COORDINATE SYSTEM
------------------------------

Unlike a tiny island, Mumbai requires true geographic travel distance for realistic road, boat, helicopter, and aeroplane operation. Use 1:1 local metre scale.

- One projected metre equals one game metre.
- Humans, roads, buildings, cars, boats, helicopters, and aeroplanes use real-world-scale dimensions.
- Do not globally compress Mumbai into a few hundred metres.
- Real airport runways remain real length; do not use hidden takeoff impulses or airport warping.

Coordinate strategy:

- Store master geographic positions in double precision.
- Project WGS84 into a suitable local East-North-Up system or verified UTM zone for Mumbai.
- Keep a documented geodetic origin.
- Render and simulate near the player in float local coordinates.
- Use a floating origin: when the focus moves beyond a tested threshold, shift render/physics origins while preserving double-precision global positions.
- Rebase terrain chunks, dynamic bodies, cameras, particles, audio, AI paths, and water consistently.
- Never pass raw latitude/longitude directly to the physics engine.

Floating-origin tests must verify:

- No visible teleport or velocity change during rebasing.
- Constraints and occupied vehicles survive a shift.
- Boat wave phase remains stable.
- Helicopter/aeroplane controls remain continuous.
- Traffic, trains, and pedestrians preserve route progress.
- Save/load round-trips global positions without kilometre-scale drift.

9. FIXED-STEP SIMULATION FOUNDATION
-----------------------------------

All local physics uses SI units:

- Metres, kilograms, seconds.
- Metres/second internally.
- Newtons and newton-metres.
- km/h or knots only for HUD presentation.

Use a fixed 60 Hz physics timestep independent from rendering:

    fixedDt = 1 / 60 second
    accumulator += min(realFrameDt, 0.1)
    while accumulator >= fixedDt and substeps < 4:
        sample buffered controls
        apply forces/torques
        step Bullet by fixedDt
        synchronize gameplay state
        accumulator -= fixedDt
    render using interpolation alpha = accumulator / fixedDt

Do not apply variable-frame force calculations to a fixed-step solver. Separate pure force models from Bullet adapters for unit testing.

Physics architecture:

- Collision layers for terrain, static architecture, human, traffic, car, boat, helicopter, aeroplane, trains, water triggers, interaction triggers, and debris.
- Static triangle meshes only for terrain/architecture.
- Convex/compound collision for dynamic vehicles.
- Continuous collision detection or swept tests for aircraft/fast vehicles.
- Sleeping for inactive bodies.
- Full simulation only near the focus/occupied vehicle; distant traffic and transit use lower-cost route simulation.
- No per-frame creation of physics shapes or large arrays.
- Origin rebasing must update physics broadphase safely.


10. HUMAN PHYSICS AND ANIMATION
-------------------------------

Implement a hybrid physical character, not a floating/sliding camera.

Controller requirements:

- Human-scale capsule, approximately 1.7–1.9 m.
- Stable ground sweep/raycast state.
- Acceleration/deceleration for walk, jog, and sprint.
- Jump based on target height:

      jumpVelocity = sqrt(2 * gravity * desiredJumpHeight)

- Limited air control.
- Slope limit, controlled sliding, curb/step handling, head clearance, moving surfaces, and ledge rejection.
- Surface response for dry/wet asphalt, dirt, grass, sand, mud, stairs, rail platforms, shallow water, and steep terrain.
- Wade and swim modes; buoyancy/stroke approximation in deep navigable water.
- Fall/hard-landing response based on impact velocity.
- Bounded impulses against lightweight bodies without launching cars or crowd agents.
- Vehicle entry/exit through validated seat and exit transforms.
- Prevent entry through walls, moving rotors/propellers, deep water, or unsafe traffic-side exits.

11. CAR PHYSICS AND LEFT-HAND URBAN DRIVING
-------------------------------------------

Create an original compact city car/4x4 suitable for Mumbai traffic.

Required physical model:

- Realistic chassis mass, dimensions, wheelbase, center of mass, and inertia.
- Four raycast or validated physical wheels.
- Per-wheel spring/damper suspension:

      springForce = springRate * compression
      damperForce = damperRate * relativeSuspensionVelocity
      normalLoad  = clamp(springForce - damperForce, 0, maximumLoad)

- Wheel inertia and angular velocity.
- Engine torque curve versus RPM.
- Idle, redline, throttle response, engine braking, clutch approximation, forward gears, neutral, reverse, final drive, and differential.
- Speed-sensitive steering and approximate Ackermann geometry.
- Longitudinal slip ratio and lateral slip angle.
- Saturating tire-force curves and friction ellipse/circle.
- Dry/wet asphalt, painted marking, concrete, dirt, grass, sand, mud, and standing-water grip.
- Brake balance, rear handbrake, optional bounded ABS/traction assists.
- Rolling resistance and aerodynamic drag.
- Stable anti-roll behavior without locking chassis orientation.
- Pothole/uneven-road impulses bounded for playability.
- Damage state that can affect alignment/engine without soft-body simulation.

Visual/audio integration:

- Physical wheel rotation, steering, and suspension travel.
- Brake/reverse/head lights.
- Rain/water spray, dust, mud, and tire marks with strict particle limits.
- Engine/gear/tire/suspension audio driven by RPM, slip, load, and surface.

Mumbai driving requirements:

- Left-hand traffic.
- Lane graph respects one-way and turn restrictions.
- Dense roads require low-speed control and progressive steering.
- Major highways allow stable higher speed.
- Flyovers/tunnels/bridges preserve correct layer separation.
- Traffic-side vehicle exit checks.

Car tests:

- Suspension settles without endless oscillation.
- Plausible acceleration through gears.
- Documented stopping distance from 50 km/h on dry and wet surfaces.
- Progressive understeer/oversteer.
- Reduced grip in rain/mud.
- Stable bridge/flyover transitions.
- No tunneling through city collision at maximum speed.
- Physics remains stable when rendering is 20–30 FPS.


12. BOAT, TIDE, AND HARBOR PHYSICS
----------------------------------

Create an original small Mumbai harbor/work/fishing boat or center-console craft.

Required model:

- Rigid hull with 6–12 distributed buoyancy points.
- Each point samples analytic water height and normal.
- Buoyancy from displaced volume:

      buoyancy = waterDensity * displacedVolume * gravity

- Apply buoyancy at sample points to create natural pitch/roll.
- Vertical damping to remove energy rather than add it.
- Directional hydrodynamic drag: forward, lateral, vertical.
- Propeller thrust based on throttle, RPM, submersion, and speed.
- Rudder/vectored-thrust steering dependent on water flow.
- Planing lift approximation at speed, strictly clamped.
- Wind drag above water.
- Tide/current zones for Arabian Sea, harbor, and creeks using documented simplified data.
- Wave spectrum differences between open sea, harbor, creek, and protected water.
- Shallow-water, mudflat, collision, and beaching behavior.
- Engine thrust reduction if propeller exits water or strikes shallow terrain.
- Wake/spray/foam tied to physical speed and hull loading.

Boat gameplay locations:

- At least one safe western coastal launch/jetty.
- At least one Mumbai Harbour/port-side public launch area.
- Optional creek route where depth/navigation data supports it.
- Clear restricted/non-navigable water barriers for reservoirs and unsuitable channels.

Boat tests:

- Correct designed waterline and stable equilibrium.
- Wave-driven pitch/roll that damps naturally.
- Better steering while moving than stationary unless thrust is vectored.
- Natural coasting after throttle release.
- Current/tide affects drift without overwhelming controls.
- Beaching does not fall through terrain or climb vertical seawalls.
- No energy explosion from overlapping buoyancy/damping.
- Boat and visible waves remain synchronized through origin rebases.


13. HELICOPTER PHYSICS IN AN URBAN COASTAL ENVIRONMENT
------------------------------------------------------

Create an original light utility helicopter.

Required model:

- Fuselage mass/inertia/center of mass.
- Main rotor and tail rotor positions, radius, RPM, inertia, and spool state.
- Collective controls thrust magnitude.
- Cyclic tilts rotor thrust and creates pitch/roll moments.
- Main rotor reaction torque acts on fuselage.
- Tail rotor/anti-torque controls yaw.
- Translational lift as horizontal airflow develops.
- Ground effect fading smoothly with rotor height and surface extent.
- Parasite drag and bounded high-speed control loss.
- Simplified vortex-ring/settling-with-power effect for steep low-forward-speed descent.
- Engine loss and simplified autorotation/rotor decay.
- Skid contact/compliance and hard-landing damage.
- Wind, coastal gust, and simplified building-wake turbulence near tall structures.
- Optional stability augmentation uses bounded torques, never position/velocity locking.

Starting relation:

      thrustMagnitude = Ct * airDensity * rotorArea * tipSpeed^2 * collective
      thrustDirection = normalized(rotorUp + cyclicPitchTerm + cyclicRollTerm)
      mainRotorForce  = thrustDirection * thrustMagnitude

Keep all coefficients in a named HelicopterConfig with units and comments.

Urban safety/gameplay:

- Spawn at a legal game-design helipad/open-airfield area, using fictional operations if necessary.
- Rotor collision/danger volume.
- Prevent exit while airborne or rotors are dangerously active.
- High-rise collisions use coarse but reliable hulls.
- Stream collision/massing far enough ahead for current airspeed.
- Public map geometry only; do not add operationally sensitive rooftop/security details.

Helicopter tests:

- Rotor spool required before takeoff.
- Force-based hover around documented collective.
- Cyclic accelerates by tilting thrust.
- Pedal counters reaction torque.
- Translational lift and ground effect are noticeable but bounded.
- Stable gentle skid landing and damaging hard landing.
- Engine loss causes descent/rotor behavior rather than suspended flight.
- North-south flight does not outrun world streaming.
- Floating-origin shift preserves velocity/orientation/control.


14. AEROPLANE PHYSICS AND REAL AIRPORTS
---------------------------------------

Create an original light single-engine propeller aeroplane suitable for realistic operations from Juhu Aerodrome and/or public-surface CSMIA runway geometry. Do not use an airliner unless its complexity and runway operations are fully implemented and legally/technically appropriate.

Required model:

- Fuselage mass, center of mass, inertia.
- Wing area/span/chord and aerodynamic center.
- Horizontal/vertical stabilizers.
- Propeller/engine.
- Three landing-gear contact points with suspension, wheel friction, steering, brakes, and visual compression.
- Local airflow from global velocity, angular velocity, wind, and optional prop wash.

Dynamic pressure:

      q = 0.5 * airDensity * airspeed^2

Lift:

      lift = q * wingArea * Cl(angleOfAttack, flapSetting)

Drag:

      drag = q * wingArea * (Cd0 + inducedDragFactor * Cl^2)

Requirements:

- Cl rises in the normal range, peaks near stall, and decreases after stall.
- Separate wing/tail/yaw contributions where practical.
- Elevator, aileron, rudder, trim, and flap response.
- Propeller thrust versus engine RPM, throttle, and airspeed.
- Ground effect near runway.
- Crosswind from the shared weather system.
- Rolling friction, braking, nose/tail steering, and gear damping.
- Bounded overspeed, excessive-G, and hard-landing damage.
- Continuous collision detection/swept checks at speed.
- No hidden upward impulse, position setting, or minimum-altitude lock.

Airport/world requirements:

- Keep real projected runway lengths and headings at 1:1 scale.
- Preserve public exterior runway/taxiway/apron geometry and markings from legal open data.
- Do not model restricted interiors or operational security systems.
- Use fictional aircraft registration/livery and gameplay operations.
- The streamer must load terrain/building massing several kilometres ahead of flight.
- Provide sufficient sea/airspace buffer for circuits and approach.

Aeroplane tests:

- Physical runway acceleration and wheel behavior.
- Takeoff only after sufficient airspeed/lift.
- Documented ground roll and climb behavior.
- Stall at high angle-of-attack/low speed, reduced control, bounded recoverability.
- Aileron roll, elevator pitch, rudder yaw.
- Flaps reduce takeoff/landing speed and increase drag.
- Crosswind affects takeoff/landing.
- Gear compression and braking on landing.
- Engine-off glide behavior.
- North/south and sea circuits do not outrun chunks or break floating origin.

18. THERMAL, BATTERY, AND PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Excessive phone heat is a release blocker even if APK size is unrestricted.

Frame pacing:

- Never default to an unrestricted 60/90/120 FPS loop.
- Balanced default target: approximately 30 FPS.
- Battery saver or moderate thermal status: approximately 20 FPS ECO.
- Severe thermal status: approximately 15 FPS.
- Pause rendering/physics/streaming correctly when Activity pauses or screen turns off.
- Respect Android screen timeout rather than forcing unattended GPU use.
- Poll thermal state at low frequency or use a listener, not a system call every frame.

ECO must reduce work, not only FPS:

- Lower dynamic resolution.
- Disable/simplify dynamic shadow sampling.
- Shorten high-detail/vegetation/particle/light ranges.
- Reduce traffic/pedestrian/transit caps and update rates.
- Reduce reflection, rain, and window-light updates.
- Use more aggressive building/vehicle LOD.
- Preserve occupied-vehicle physics stability and immediate controls.

Suggested balanced budgets:

- Frame: 33.3 ms at 30 FPS.
- Main simulation/game logic: ideally below 6–8 ms average.
- Draw calls: preferably below 150–250 depending on device.
- Visible triangles: roughly 200k–500k after LOD, verified by profiling.
- Nearby fully simulated dynamic bodies: below approximately 100–150.
- One main directional shadow system.
- Texture residency target: approximately 256–384 MB on balanced, lower on LOW/ECO.
- No unbounded Java/native heap growth.
- No major per-frame allocations/GC spikes.

Required optimization:

- Hierarchical chunk streaming.
- Frustum/distance/sector culling.
- Static batching by chunk/material.
- Mesh and collision LOD.
- Texture atlases, mipmaps, compression, shared materials.
- Instancing/impostors for vegetation/repeated props.
- Object pools for particles/agents.
- Sleeping/proxy simulation for distant physics.
- Altitude-aware city detail for aircraft.
- Bounded asynchronous decoding/GPU upload.
- Development diagnostics overlay: FPS, CPU/GPU frame time, draw calls, triangles, chunks, pending loads, RAM, texture bytes, active bodies/traffic/pedestrians, and thermal profile.

Profiles:

LOW:
- Short near-detail ranges, aggressive massing LOD, low particles/lights, reduced traffic, 512/no shadow depending on capability.

BALANCED:
- Default 30 FPS, compact throttled shadows, moderate city rings, normal material set.

HIGH:
- Better shadows/LOD/reflections/city lights, still protected by thermal override.

ECO:
- Automatic battery/thermal override visibly shown in HUD and not bypassable at severe temperature.

APK install size does not excuse high runtime memory, GPU bandwidth, or thermal load.
